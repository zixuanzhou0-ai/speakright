## Evidence and claim limits

- Screenshot and demo fixtures are labelled example data and make no live paid
  TTS or Azure scoring call.
- Automated, synthetic, and maintainer sessions are not counted as users.
  [`USER_TESTING_SUMMARY.md`](USER_TESTING_SUMMARY.md) remains pending real,
  consented, anonymized aggregates.
- American English is the stable learning baseline. Spanish, French, and
  Russian remain experimental.
- Numeric production scores come from the user's Azure service; configured LLMs
  explain evidence and do not create the numeric score.
- The Desktop Preview is unsigned. The bare EXE and NSIS setup may be published
  only with their warning, checksums, SBOMs, and validation reports. MSI is not
  a v1.1.0 public artifact.
- The reviewed NSIS preflight has a narrow process-name check-to-launch timing
  window; the release workflow therefore runs the mutation only on a fresh,
  isolated GitHub-hosted Windows runner.
